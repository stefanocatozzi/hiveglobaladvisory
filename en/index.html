Common questions

What services does HIVE Global Advisory provide? +
We provide integrated legal, tax and financial advisory services for cross-border business and private clients, spanning corporate & commercial law, international taxation, wealth management, and M&A & due diligence. Our team coordinates across disciplines so you have a single point of contact for complex, multi-jurisdiction matters.

We operate directly from our offices in Shanghai, Ho Chi Minh City, Mumbai, Dubai and Florence, and work through a vetted network of correspondent firms across North America, Latin America, the Middle East, Europe and Asia-Pacific. See our Global presence page for the full picture.
